## Overview
Cadre AI is an AI strategy and implementation consultancy. They help
businesses move from AI confusion to AI confidence — identifying
high-ROI AI opportunities department by department, building workflows
and agents, and training teams so the changes stick. Positioning: "The
companies that win with AI are not the ones with the most tools. They're
the ones with a plan." (Source: challenge brief; cadreai.com home)

## Industries served
B2B companies across: Professional Services, Private Equity, Financial
Services, Mortgage & Lending, Real Estate, Construction, Manufacturing &
Logistics, Retail & E-commerce, Hospitality. Also works across
departments: Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, Executive Leadership,
Finance, Operations, Technology, Legal. (Source: challenge brief;
cadreai.com/industries)

## Core services (four)
1. **AI Strategy** — goes from "zero clarity to a prioritized roadmap."
   Runs a "45-Day AI Transformation Intensive" (discover use cases →
   survey the landscape → implement → scale), focused on quick wins with
   measurable EBITDA impact. (cadreai.com/strategy)
2. **AI Leadership & Facilitation** — turns teams into "AI champions"
   through experiential (not lecture-based) training: 30% Teaching / 30%
   Interaction / 40% Application. Formats: 2-Day Leadership Intensive,
   1-Day Workshop, Half-Day Executive Session, 1-Hour Virtual Kickoff.
   (cadreai.com/leadership-facilitation)
3. **AI Engineering** — orchestrates intelligent processes: automates
   data entry, document routing, and reporting; connects systems via
   API; adds context-aware AI routing; deploys custom agents.
   (cadreai.com/ai-engineering)
4. **AI Agents** — autonomous workers across three tiers: Prompts &
   Assistants, Voice Agents, and Fully Fledged AI Agents (plan + act
   across tools, with guardrails and human oversight). (cadreai.com/agents)

## The AI Maturity Index
Scores a company across an **eight-pillar framework** for AI
transformation, giving a grade in each area with explanations and
actionable next steps. The eight pillars: Dedicated AI Team, AI Command
Center, AI-First Culture Shift, AI-Enabled Tech Stack, AI-Healthy Data,
AI Agent Readiness, Departmental Deep Dives, Three-Year AI Vision.
Accessed via a "Get Your AI Maturity Index" call to action, which leads
to the contact page. **Not published on the site:** the exact scoring
mechanics (e.g. self-serve quiz vs. consultant-led). If asked for that
level of detail, escalate. (cadreai.com/strategy, cadreai.com/contact)
